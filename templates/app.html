Launch App

Everything runs in your browser. No server sees your keys. Dry runs are free — credits are only used on live sweeps.

Security: This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your mnemonics are never sent to a server. Verify by opening your browser's DevTools → Network tab — you'll see no requests carrying your keys.

1. Wallet source

One mnemonic per line. BIP-39 (12 or 24 words).

Supports EVM + Solana + Bitcoin derivations from the same phrase.

Scan the QR with your mobile wallet, or approve in your browser extension.

Note: WalletConnect supports EVM chains only. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

Connect your Ledger, open the Ethereum app, then click Connect. Requires Chrome or Edge (WebHID).

Note: Hardware wallets currently support EVM chains only.

⚠ Before running a live sweep:
  • Make sure each wallet has enough native gas:
  • Ethereum mainnet: ~0.005 ETH
    Base / Arbitrum / Optimism: ~0.0002 ETH
    Polygon: ~0.5 POL
  • Wallets without gas will fail on that chain.
  • Credits are consumed whether or not the sweep succeeds.
