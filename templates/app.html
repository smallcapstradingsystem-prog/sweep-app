Sweep

Consolidate any number of wallets into USDC at a destination you choose. Everything runs in your browser — no phrase is ever transmitted. Preview is free.

Client-side signing. Every source phrase and your destination are processed locally. Derivation, signing, and broadcast all happen on your device. Verify with DevTools → Network — no request carries your keys.

1. Wallet source

Pick how you want to provide your source wallets. Every method signs locally — nothing leaves your device.

One mnemonic per line. BIP-39 (12 or 24 words).

Supports EVM + Solana + Bitcoin derivations from the same phrase.

Connect with the wallet extension installed in your browser. Works with MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Brave Wallet, and others.

For multi-chain sweeps, MetaMask, Rabby, or Coinbase Wallet work best. Phantom and some other wallets only support one chain at a time, so sweeps across multiple EVM chains may skip chains that the wallet can't switch to. For the most reliable cross-chain sweep, use the mnemonic method.

Note: Browser extensions support EVM chains only. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

Scan the QR with your mobile wallet, or approve in your browser extension.

Note: WalletConnect supports EVM chains only. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

Connect your Ledger, open the Ethereum app, then click Connect. Requires Chrome or Edge (WebHID).

Note: Hardware wallets currently support EVM chains only.

Connect your Trezor, then click Connect. A Trezor popup will appear for approval.

Note: Hardware wallets currently support EVM chains only.

2. Destination

The wallet that receives the swept USDC. Use a different address than the ones you're sweeping — the sweep wallets get emptied. EVM, Solana, and Bitcoin sweeps all deliver USDC to this address.

Solana and Bitcoin sweeps are bridged to Ethereum USDC and delivered here.

3. Families

Uncheck a family to leave it alone. Preview only shows what you check.

4. Mode

How the fee works. 10% of the swept value is taken at swap time by the underlying routing protocol. 90% lands directly at your destination in the same transaction. Preview is always free.
