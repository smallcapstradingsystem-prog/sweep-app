FAQ

Do you ever see my seed phrase?

No. Never. The mnemonic is entered into an HTML form and processed entirely in JavaScript. The signing happens in your browser.

How do I verify that?

Open DevTools → Network. Paste a mnemonic. Click Preview. Search every request for a word from your phrase. You'll find nothing.

Why $5 per sweep?

Server costs, RPC fees, and the maintenance of a tool that has to work across five EVM chains plus Solana plus Bitcoin. It's a one-time fee, not a subscription.

Can I pay with crypto?

Yes. USDC on Base (cheapest), USDC on Ethereum, ETH on Base, SOL, or BTC. You'll be shown an address and an exact amount to send.

What if I don't use my credit?

It stays in your browser until you use it. No expiration.

Is this legal?

This is non-custodial software. It does not hold your funds, does not sign on your behalf, and does not provide financial advice. You are responsible for the transactions you sign.
