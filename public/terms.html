5. Service fee

Live sweeps incur a 10% service fee on the swept value. The fee is collected atomically at swap time by the routing protocol and delivered to our fee wallet. You receive the remaining 90% directly at your destination address in the same transaction.

There is no up-front payment, no account, and no prepaid credit balance. The fee is taken from the swept value itself, so you cannot lose money you didn't sweep.

6. Gas sponsorship

For EVM source wallets that do not hold enough native token to cover the sweep, we may sponsor a small amount of native gas. Sponsorship is discretionary and rate-limited. When we sponsor gas, we record the sponsorship for accounting purposes and may deduct an equivalent amount from the user's share of the swept value before forwarding, at the rates described on our site.

Solana and Bitcoin sweeping are not gas-sponsored. Solana source wallets must hold a small SOL buffer; Bitcoin network fees are deducted from the swept UTXOs.
