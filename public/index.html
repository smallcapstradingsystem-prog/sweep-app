How it works

Four steps. One sweep.

Every transaction is signed and broadcast from your browser. PoolPort LiquiFi's servers never touch your keys, your funds, or your phrases.

FAQ

Questions.

Does PoolPort LiquiFi ever see my seed phrase?

No. Phrase handling runs entirely in your browser — derivation, signing, and broadcast. No request carries any part of a mnemonic. You can verify this yourself: open DevTools, click the Network tab, run a preview, and inspect every request. None of them contains your phrase.

What does it cost?

A 10% service fee is taken at swap time by the underlying routing protocol (0x on EVM, deBridge on Solana, THORChain on Bitcoin). 90% of the swept value is delivered directly to your destination address in the same transaction. There is no up-front payment, no account, and no credits to buy. Preview is free.

How does gas work? Do I need ETH in my sweep wallets?

No. We sponsor gas automatically for EVM wallets that don't have enough native token (ETH, BNB, POL) to run the sweep. You don't need to pre-fund your sweep wallets. Solana source wallets still need a small SOL balance for the bridge order fee, and Bitcoin fees are deducted from the swept UTXOs.

Which chains are supported?

Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Polygon, and BNB Chain for EVM. Plus Solana and Bitcoin as their own families. That's eight chains across three families in a single sweep flow.

How many wallets can I sweep?

As many as you have. Paste multiple mnemonics, one per line, and they all sweep in sequence. There is no limit and no per-wallet charge beyond the 10% fee on each wallet's swept value.

What if a token has no route to USDC?

It's skipped. You'll see it in the preview with a "no route" label. The token stays in your wallet untouched — spam airdrops and illiquid tokens are left alone by design.

Can I use this with a hardware wallet?

Yes, on EVM chains — Ledger (via WebHID) and Trezor are supported. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping currently require a mnemonic.

Why isn't the source code public?

The fee and routing logic is the core of the product, and client-side bundles are trivially decompiled. We've laid out the full reasoning — and what you can still verify without source — at /private-source.html. Security researchers can request NDA access to the private repo.

Is this legal?

This is non-custodial software. It does not hold your funds, does not sign on your behalf, and does not provide financial advice. You are responsible for the transactions you sign.
